# Pendola Pass map draft — 2026-10-12

Own 3072 × 3072 schematic drawing, generated by `scripts/render-pendola-pass.py` using Pillow. No screenshot pixels, filters, overlays or original map assets are included. Four user-provided German in-game map screenshots were visually inspected as references. No game data was researched or asserted from external sources.

## Geographic status: PARTIALLY VERIFIED

The four main German area names and 16 additional names were read from the supplied screenshots. Relative arrangement was visually compared: Almatera west of the central channel, observatory northeast, reception/station east of the channel, loading platforms southeast, rest stop southwest of old town, substation east of old town.

The drawing is **not a geographically verified reconstruction**. Positions are visually estimated, not registered through common control points. Photo perspective, cropping, glare and screen patterns prevent exact measurement. Screenshot margins do not establish the entire playable boundary. There is no verified world scale or elevation model.

Known deviations: very simplified valley walls; no exact terrain relief, snow textures or individual rocks/trees; building groups use generalized roofs rather than surveyed footprints; road and rail bends are approximations; small connections and obscured areas remain unresolved; the southern extent is estimated using the zoom screenshot. The two telescope pads and loading roofs are schematic infrastructure drawings, not loot markers. German names are embedded in the map; the surrounding draft notice supports DE/EN/FR/ES/IT. Official translated place names have not been verified.

The screenshot-visible western village consists of large roof groups; further small structures are omitted. The map is suitable for reviewing the new map entry and controls, **not for precision navigation or placing gameplay markers**. More accurate manual tracing/registration and a geographic review are open work before any claim of original fidelity.
