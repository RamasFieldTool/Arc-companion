- Dam exterior: possible elevated concrete position in the central exterior section.
- The Breach: possible position on collapsed grated flooring on the north side.
- Pipeline Tower: multiple rooftop candidates; zipline/mobility is useful for roof access.
- Hydroponic Dome Complex: several possible interior candidates, including east and southwest portions.
- East of Power Generation Complex: possible ground-level candidate near electrical systems.
- East Broken Bridge: possible candidate under the broken bridge in a small concrete alcove.
- Floodgates: multiple sources report possible cases around/inside the floodgate passage. A separate breachable-room route is also reported after lowering a bridge from the dam/control-tower side.

## Data policy
- Weapon Cases remain OFF by default.
- All markers say possible spawn; no ordinary marker is a guaranteed case.
- Reported totals are community counts, not official totals.
- POI/access descriptions can be stored before coordinates.
- A point is rendered only when x/y can be responsibly transferred to the app map.
- Missing or conflicting access details are omitted rather than guessed.
