A separate simplified app-icon concept was approved by Daniel Rogge on 17 September 2026.

- exactly three stripes integrated into the right side of the A: yellow, orange and red,

## Derivative rules

Permitted preparation:

- exact resizing and format normalization,
- adaptive-icon safe-zone positioning,
- separating the selected icon into foreground/background layers,
- creation of a simplified monochrome launcher layer,
- removal of the tiny FIELD TOOL text from launcher-only layers when needed for small-size legibility, while retaining the approved A, stripes and pin.

Not permitted without explicit approval:

- changing the three-stripe color order,
- removing the A or map-pin concept,
- adding unrelated symbols or wording,
- presenting the app as an official Embark Studios product.

Every production derivative must be visually compared with the selected icon and recorded with a SHA-256 checksum.
