# Android and Google Play asset requirements

Last official-requirements check: 17 September 2026.

## Two-part branding rule

### Full marketing logo

Keep the detailed approved Ramas Field Tool / ARC Companion badge as the full marketing artwork. It contains the Raider scene, ARC COMPANION title, slogan and feature symbols. Its source record remains in `LOGO_SOURCE.md`.

### App icon

Use the separately approved simplified app-icon concept for Android and Google Play:

- distressed cream A,
- exactly three integrated stripes: yellow, orange and red,
- subtle map pin inside the A,
- small FIELD TOOL lettering,
- dark background,
- circular distressed cream frame.

The simplified app icon is an approved derivative identity. It does not replace the detailed marketing logo.

Do not:

- use an unrelated ARC Raiders logo,
- imply official affiliation with Embark Studios,
- add official-store badges, rankings or misleading claims,
- silently change the selected app-icon design.

## Current official Google Play requirements

Google Play icon:

- 32-bit PNG with alpha,
- exactly 512 × 512 px,
- maximum 1,024 KB,
- separate from the installed launcher-icon resources.

Feature graphic:

- JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha,
- exactly 1024 × 500 px,
- keep the focal point and essential content away from crop zones.

Phone screenshots:

- at least two screenshots are required across supported device types,
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha,
- each dimension between 320 and 3,840 px,
- the long side must not exceed twice the short side,
- four 1080 × 1920 portrait screenshots are recommended for promotional eligibility.

These values must be rechecked in Play Console immediately before submission.
