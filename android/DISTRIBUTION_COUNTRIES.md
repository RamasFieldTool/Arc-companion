# Initial Google Play distribution countries

Decision date: 17 September 2026  
Strategy: first public release limited to German- and English-language markets.

## Phase 1 countries

| Code | Country | Primary supported listing |
|---|---|---|
| CH | Switzerland | German / English |
| DE | Germany | German |
| AT | Austria | German |
| LI | Liechtenstein | German |
| US | United States | English |
| CA | Canada | English |
| GB | United Kingdom | English |
| IE | Ireland | English |
| AU | Australia | English |
| NZ | New Zealand | English |

## Reasoning

- Matches the app's confirmed German and English interfaces and store listings.
- Includes Daniel's home market, Switzerland.
- Provides meaningful reach without starting worldwide.
- Keeps early support and review feedback manageable.
- Countries may be added later without changing the application ID.

## Play Console procedure

Before public release:

1. Select exactly the ten Phase 1 countries in Play Console.
2. Confirm that the German listing is active for German-language users.
3. Confirm that the English listing is the default for all other selected markets.
4. Recheck whether any country-specific declarations are shown by Play Console.
5. Do not expand distribution until the first release has passed technical and user-feedback review.

Internal and closed testing remain governed by their tester lists and track settings. Public country availability must not be used as a substitute for controlled testing.
